# E-waste and unused equipment

What happens to computing equipment our group no longer uses, and how to pass it on, reuse it or dispose of it properly.

Making hardware has a large environmental cost, often more than running it. So the first choice is always to keep equipment in use for longer, then to pass it on, and only then to recycle it.

## Before you get rid of anything

1. **Could someone else use it?** Ask the group first, then the wider department.
2. **Can it be repaired or upgraded?** A new battery, more memory or a fresh operating system often extends a laptop's life by years.
3. **Wipe your data**, following IT's instructions, before it leaves your hands.
4. **Update your Green DiSC record**: set the item's status to `unused` or `disposed`, and say in **Action** what will happen or happened.
